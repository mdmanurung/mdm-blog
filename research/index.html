Research

Questions connecting population immunology, infection and vaccination, and computational methods.

My research connects experimental immunology with computational analysis to understand differences in human immune responses.

Human immune variation

How do environment, ancestry and infection history shape immunity?

People encounter different environments and microbes throughout life. Understanding these histories can help us interpret differences in immune profiles across populations.

How I study it

I combine population studies, high-dimensional immune phenotyping and antibody repertoire profiling to study variation across rural and urban settings.

What we’re learning

Our systems analysis identified a rural–urban gradient in immune profiles, function and metabolic measurements across populations. Our PhIP-seq study adds complementary information about microbial exposure histories.

Infection & vaccination

Which immune responses contribute to protection?

The same infection or vaccine can elicit different responses between people. Controlled infection studies help us observe how those responses develop over time.

How I study it

I analyse longitudinal immune measurements from vaccination and controlled human infection studies, alongside comparisons with naturally exposed populations.

What we’re learning

Our controlled hookworm study followed immune remodelling in four volunteers and compared it with profiles in endemic populations. This provides a longitudinal example of how infection can reshape immune-cell profiles.

Computational immunology

How can complex immune measurements become interpretable evidence?

Single-cell and multi-omics experiments generate many measurements. The challenge is to turn those measurements into interpretable patterns that can be checked and reused.

How I study it

I develop and apply methods for cell-type-specific expression programs, multiblock integration and reproducible analysis workflows.

What we’re learning

The software collection spans interpretable analysis and research practice: cellprograms for expression programs, stablr for biomarker selection, RaJIVEplus for multiblock variation and ViralScan for viral signals. Mycelium Extra supports planning and oversight of agent-assisted analyses.
